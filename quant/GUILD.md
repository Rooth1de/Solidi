# Guild operating mode (permanent until live) — 2026-11-11
Sequence (no skipping; a skipped role voids the idea): Ranger -> Seer -> Alchemist -> Paladin -> Guild Master.
Ranger: raw ideas/catalysts only. Seer: converts to a concrete setup (instrument, trigger, exit, residual, payer).
Alchemist: pre-registers and tests under RULES.md R0/R1/R2; reports survive/die; no approval power.
Paladin: independent risk veto (risk, regime, crowding, tail, process); veto is final.
Guild Master: enforces sequence, records status: REJECTED / WATCHLIST / APPROVED-FOR-PAPER; escalates real decisions only.
No execution role (Rogue) until live. Decision record: logs/GUILD_LOG.md.
